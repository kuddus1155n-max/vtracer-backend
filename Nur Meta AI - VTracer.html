Low creates simpler vectors with fewer paths and anchor points. High preserves more of the image's fine detail.

Medium
Controls how aggressively traced paths are simplified and smoothed after tracing. Low keeps paths close to the raw trace with more anchor points and sharper corners; high removes more anchor points and rounds curves into flowing Bezier arcs. Real sharp corners are always kept crisp either way.
Remove background
Automatically detects a flat white (or other solid-color) background around the edges of the image and makes it transparent before tracing, so the SVG contains just the artwork — no background rectangle behind it. Safely does nothing if the image doesn't have a flat, removable background (e.g. a busy photo).
30
Limit number of colors
Quantizes the image to an exact palette size before tracing, so you get precise control over how many colors the vector uses (approx. 2–32). Overrides the color count that "Detail level" would normally pick. Requires "Simplify shapes before tracing", which turns on automatically while this is enabled.
16
Approximate — VTracer's own tracing can occasionally merge or drop a color that covers a tiny area.
Simplify shapes before tracing
Detects the major shapes/edges first and removes pixel-level noise, JPEG artifacts, and gradient banding — so VTracer traces clean flat regions instead of every pixel. Turn off to trace the raw image as-is.
Clean up SVG paths
After tracing: removes duplicate paths, tiny stray objects, broken/degenerate paths, and unnecessary overlaps, and collapses redundant anchor points. Turn off to keep VTracer's raw output as-is.
SVG keeps every path fully editable and opens cleanly in Adobe Illustrator. EPS is generated from the same paths (flat vector shapes, no rasterizing) for tools or marketplaces that specifically require .eps.
Vector Quality Check
Scans the traced SVG for anchor-point bloat, stray "confetti" objects, duplicate paths, open paths that should be closed, and broken/degenerate path data — so you know if the result is clean before you download it.
—
Color Segmentation
Detects the image's main colors and splits the traced result into a separate vector shape group per color — useful for multi-layer laser cutting, screen-print separations, or per-color editing in another tool.
0.5%
Colors covering less of the image than this are treated as noise/speckle, not a "main color".
